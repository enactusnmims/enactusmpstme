What We Do

Plastic Waste to Wearable Art

Shilpkaar creates plarn - yarn made from upcycled plastic bags - and trains rural craftswomen to weave it into beautiful, functional products using Gujarat's authentic kala cotton tradition. The result: waterproof, durable, handcrafted bags that are 27-30% cheaper than competitors.

93 paise per rupee earned flows directly to artisans - one of the highest artisan-income ratios in the industry.
Products are listed on Amazon and Flipkart, and available at projectshilpkaar.com. Revenue split 60/40 between B2B and B2C.
Handcrafted using Gujarat's authentic kala cotton, combining modern design with traditional weaving craft.

Production Process

How it's Made

01
Collection

Collecting and sanitizing single-use plastic bags that would otherwise end up in landfills or oceans.

02
Spinning

Meticulously cutting and spinning the plastic into "plarn" - a durable, waterproof upcycled yarn.

03
Weaving

Rural artisans weave the plarn using traditional kala cotton looms to create unique, hand-crafted fabric.

04
Finishing

The fabric is tailored into premium bags, quality-checked, and made ready for global marketplaces.
