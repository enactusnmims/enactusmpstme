The Problem

Why AgriLink 360 Exists

India's 126 million smallholder farmers face a broken system. Without direct market access, they rely on exploitative middlemen and earn only 10% of their crop's actual sale value.

Only 10% Earnings
Farmers relying on middlemen earn only 10% of their crop's actual sale value - the rest is captured by intermediaries.
75% Uninsured
Three out of four farming families lack crop insurance or financial support, making them highly vulnerable to unpredictable risks.
$18.5B Annual Losses
India loses over 15% of select crops each year after harvest due to pests and weather, causing $18.5 billion in losses annually.
Four Pillars

How We Solve It

One Digital Network
Connects 120M+ farmers with buyers, experts and fintech tools. Offline-first and multilingual.
AI Precision Insights
Real-time weather, soil and crop health data. Predict risks and protect yield before problems occur.
B2B Marketplace
Direct access to verified buyers. QR-based traceability enables ESG pricing and boosts profits by 15-20%.
Embedded FinTech
Instant microloans and insurance with no paperwork. AI credit profiling unlocks financial inclusion.
