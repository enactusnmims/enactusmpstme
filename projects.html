Our Legacy

Past Projects

Projects that have shaped our organisation and created lasting community impact.

Project Zeen
Farmer to Consumer Markets

Bridged urban communities with farmers through F2C markets across Mumbai. Rs. 29.5L+ revenue, 2,400+ beneficiaries, 45 markets, 300% income increase for farmers.

Project Parivartan
Empowering Rag Pickers

Empowered rag pickers through upcycling. 38 women trained, 150% income increase, 2,236 kg of waste repurposed into sawdust pencils and other products.

Project Vishuddh
Health and Sanitation

Empowered low-income women to manufacture eco-friendly sanitary pads and Vidhra detergent. 800+ individuals impacted across slum communities.

Project Patched
Uplifting Trafficking Survivors

3E model (Education, Entrepreneurship, Empowerment) for trafficking survivors. 28+ women helped, Rs. 5,000/month average income through patchwork craft sales.
