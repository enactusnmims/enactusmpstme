Who We Are

Turning Students Into
Changemakers

Enactus MPSTME is a student organisation at NMIMS Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management and Engineering. We blend business strategy with engineering innovation to create scalable, sustainable solutions for pressing social and environmental challenges.

We don't just raise awareness - we build ventures that create measurable livelihoods and real impact.
