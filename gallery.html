Parichay 2.0

Parichay 2.0 is the annual introductory event and recruitment kickoff hosted by Enactus MPSTME at NMIMS — the first step into the annual recruitment journey. The event brought students together through interactive games, open discussions, and social impact shopping, creating an opportunity to get to know our team, culture, and working style in a relaxed and engaging environment.
