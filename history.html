A Look Back

Our History

Over a decade of student-driven entrepreneurial action - transforming communities, one project at a time across Maharashtra and beyond.

Jump to Year
2012 - Founded 2015 - Project Zeen 2018 - Parivartan 2019 - Vishuddh 2020 - Patched 2021 - Shilpkaar 2022 - Rupaantar 2025 - AgriLink 360
2012
Enactus MPSTME Founded
Established at NMIMS Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management and Engineering, Mumbai. A group of passionate students joined the global Enactus movement - active in 36 countries - committed to using entrepreneurial action to create a better world.
2015
Project Zeen - Farmer to Consumer Markets
Project Zeen bridges the gap between urban communities and farmers by establishing F2C (Farmer to Consumer) markets across Mumbai. The project generated over Rs. 29.5 lakh in revenue, impacted 2,400+ beneficiaries, established 45 F2C markets across Malad, Thane and Kandivali, and achieved weekly sales exceeding 2.5 tonnes. Farmer incomes increased by 300%.
2018
Project Parivartan - Empowering Rag Pickers
Sought to empower rag pickers and urban slum dwellers through upcycling of waste materials - teaching skills such as creating sawdust pencils and Boweys from discarded scrap. 38 women were empowered with entrepreneurial skills, achieving a 150% increase in their income. Over 2,236 kg of waste was repurposed.
2019
Project Vishuddh - Health and Sanitation
Addressed health and sanitation problems by empowering low-income groups to become entrepreneurs. Partnered with NGOs to facilitate manufacturing and selling of eco-friendly sanitary pads and Vidhra, a non-toxic washing powder. Impact extended to 800+ individuals. Door-to-door waste collection in slums promoted hygiene and community well-being.
2020
Project Patched - Uplifting Trafficking Survivors
Dedicated to the upliftment of human trafficking victims through the 3E model - Education, Entrepreneurship and Empowerment. Women were trained in spoken English, computer skills and patchwork crafts (cushions, quilts). 28+ women were helped, with an average income of Rs. 5,000 per month and over Rs. 30,000 per year.
2021
Project Shilpkaar - Plarn Handicrafts
Launched to empower Indian craftswomen through sustainable plarn bags made from upcycled plastic. Now one of Enactus MPSTME's flagship ventures. Achievements include Rs. 6 lakh+ in sales, 80+ artisans employed, 375 kg+ of plastic upcycled and a 50% artisan income increase. Recognised by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Union Ministers and the Chief Minister of Maharashtra.
2022
Project Rupaantar - Waste to Wealth
Started with incense sticks from floral waste, pivoted through orange peel leather and rice husk clay, before arriving at the breakthrough - handmade eco-paper from rice husk priced at Rs. 5 per unit. Secured 2nd place at IIM Indore and 1st place at Kalinga Institute. Key partnerships with Pratibha Tai in Chiplun and Kalpana Paper Factory in Jaipur.
2025 - Present
AgriLink 360 - Agri-Tech Platform
AI-powered platform designed to connect 120M+ smallholder farmers with buyers, crop insights and embedded financial services. Targets the elimination of exploitative middlemen who reduce farmers to earning only 10% of their crop's value. Revenue model includes freemium B2C, B2B SaaS and fintech partnerships. Launching in the Konkan region, scaling to Maharashtra and Uttar Pradesh.
