Who We Are

About Enactus MPSTME

A decade of student-driven social entrepreneurship, empowering communities and creating lasting change across India.

Our Story

Passion Meets Purpose

Our story is one where passion meets purpose and dreams become reality. Our journey at Enactus MPSTME is a tale of students coming together, fueled by a shared desire to create meaningful change.

From the bustling halls of Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management to the heart of Mumbai, our adventure is filled with ups, downs, and everything in between. We've faced challenges head-on, learned from our mistakes, and celebrated triumphs both big and small.

But above all, our story is about people - the dedicated students, mentors, and partners who make our mission possible.

Since inception, we have tackled pressing social and environmental issues across Maharashtra by empowering underserved communities and building sustainable business models. What sets us apart is our unique interdisciplinary approach, blending business strategy with technical innovation.

How We Work

Our Approach

Rooted in collaboration, innovation, and empathy - we tackle social challenges through hands-on experience, rigorous research, and creative problem-solving.

Field Research
We embed ourselves in the communities we serve, conducting deep fieldwork to understand real needs before designing any solution.
Build & Iterate
Leveraging our engineering and management backgrounds, we prototype, test, and refine solutions for maximum impact and scalability.
Measure Impact
Every project is tracked with clear KPIs - income generated, livelihoods created, waste reduced - ensuring our work creates lasting change.
Community First
We co-create with our beneficiaries, not for them. Solutions are built with deep cultural sensitivity and community ownership.
Sustainability
Projects are designed to be financially self-sufficient over time - not dependent on grants, but powered by genuine market demand.
Global Network
As part of Enactus movement in 36 countries - we access world-class mentorship, competitions, and best practices from student entrepreneurs.
Sectors

Industries We Target

Handicrafts

Preserving traditional Indian crafts while creating fair, sustainable livelihoods for rural artisans through modern market linkages and circular production models.

Agriculture

Building technology and supply chains that help India's 126 million smallholder farmers access better markets, reduce post-harvest losses, and gain financial inclusion.

Green Materials

Converting agricultural and plastic waste into premium everyday products - eco-paper, upcycled bags - driving the circular economy at the grassroots level.

Get in Touch
Where does Enactus MPSTME work? +
We work primarily across Maharashtra and Gujarat, in communities facing economic, social, or environmental challenges. We partner with NGOs, FPOs, and local groups to understand needs and co-create sustainable solutions.
Who can benefit from Enactus projects? +
Women entrepreneurs, artisans, small-scale farmers, youth, and individuals from economically disadvantaged backgrounds are our primary beneficiaries. We focus on communities with high potential for impact through skill-building and entrepreneurship.
How can companies support us via CSR? +
Companies can partner through CSR funding, B2B procurement of project products, corporate gifting, or co-creating initiatives. Our projects create measurable, auditable social and environmental impact aligned with ESG goals.
How can students join Enactus MPSTME? +
MPSTME students can join during our annual recruitment drive at the start of the academic year. Follow our Instagram @en.mpstme for announcements. We welcome students passionate about entrepreneurship, design, marketing, tech, or social work.
How long does Enactus engage with a community? +
We aim to create self-sufficient models. Engagement continues until the community has the skills, resources, and confidence to operate independently - typically 2–4 years, with ongoing support as needed.
